Privacy Policy: How We Handle Your Enquiry Details

Last updated: October 2026. This policy explains what information we collect through exhibitionstallsdesigns.com and how we use it.

Who we are

Exhibition Stalls Designs ("we", "us") is an exhibition stall design and fabrication business based in Karachi, Pakistan. Contact: hello@exhibitionstallsdesigns.com.

Information we collect

  • Details you send us: name, phone or WhatsApp number, email, city, event details and any files or messages you provide through our forms or by contacting us.
  • Technical data: basic information your browser shares when you visit, such as device type and pages viewed, if analytics are enabled.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare a quote
  • To plan, deliver and support your project
  • To improve our website and services
  • To meet legal and accounting obligations

We do not sell your personal information.

Who processes it

Our enquiry form is delivered by a third-party form service, which passes your message to us by email. We may also use tools such as analytics, maps and fonts that load from other providers. These providers handle data under their own policies.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising cookies. If we add analytics, we will describe them here. Embedded content such as Google Maps and Google Fonts may set their own cookies.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and project information only as long as needed to deal with your request, deliver the work and meet legal or accounting requirements.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing hello@exhibitionstallsdesigns.com.

Security and changes

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, though no online method is completely secure. We may update this policy, and the date above shows the latest version.
