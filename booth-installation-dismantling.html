Booth Installation and Dismantling: What Happens on Build-Up Day

On-site assembly is where weeks of design meet a few hours of hall access. This page walks through the sequence and explains why test assembly, where we do it, makes build-up day faster and calmer.

In short: Our own crews of 5 to 10 people typically install a stall in about three days, support you on show days and dismantle it afterwards. Where we test-assemble a stall in the workshop first, build-up day follows a tested order, and 40% of payment falls due on installation and complete setup.

The install sequence

  1. Floor protection: the hall floor is covered before anything is built.
  2. Structure: frames and main walls go up in their tested order.
  3. Panels and counters: fitted, levelled and secured.
  4. Graphics: printed panels, lettering and fascias fitted.
  5. Lighting and electrics: installed and checked.
  6. Final walk-through: clean, complete and ready.

Why test assembly saves hours

When a stall is test-assembled in our workshop, which we do where the project calls for it, every part is numbered. On site, the crew rebuilds in the same order, so there is little guessing and few surprises. Problems with fit, finish or dimensions surface in the workshop, where fixing them costs minutes instead of a missed deadline.

Crew size and timing

A typical installation uses a crew of 5 to 10 people and takes about three days, depending on the stall's size and design and the access time the venue gives.

Show-day support

We do not disappear once the doors open. Our team offers show-day support, so a light, a panel or a graphic can be fixed while the exhibition runs.

Dismantling and clear-up

After the event our crew takes the stall down, removes it and leaves your space clear for the venue. Tell us the exit time early so we can plan the crew around it.

What helps us most

  • Venue name, city and your allotted build-up time
  • Stall number and position
  • Any venue access or delivery rules
  • A contact person on site

Design sign-off is needed at least one week before the event.
