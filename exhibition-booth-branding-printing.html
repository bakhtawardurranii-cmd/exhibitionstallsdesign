Exhibition Booth Branding and Printing: Which Graphic Suits Your Stall?

The same logo behaves differently on fabric, vinyl and a lit panel. This page explains how each graphic type works, so you spend on the finish that suits your stall and your audience.

In short: We print and fit booth graphics in-house, including backlit panels, fabric, vinyl, 3D lettering, floor graphics and banners, so panel sizes, edges and lighting line up with the stall that is built.

Which graphic for which job?

GraphicBest for
Backlit panelStanding out in dim or busy halls
Fabric tension graphicSeamless, crease-free walls and easy reuse
VinylCustom walls and one-off shapes
3D letteringFascias and logos seen from the aisle
Floor graphicGuiding visitors into the stall
Banners and standeesEntrances and side zones

Prepare files like a printer

  • Send logos as vector files (AI, EPS or PDF) so they stay sharp at any size.
  • Share brand colour references or a brand guide.
  • Supply final, proofread text. Errors are expensive to fix after printing.
  • Send images at high resolution, because big prints magnify blur.

The colour reality

Screens glow and paper reflects, so printed colours rarely match a monitor exactly. If a particular brand colour matters, tell us early and share a reference so we can check it against what we print.

Why in-house printing helps

Because printing, fabrication and installation share the same team, panel sizes and fixing methods are known before artwork is finalised, and a reprint does not wait on another company. Design sign-off is needed at least one week before the event. See stall design and fabrication.

Common questions

Do you print the graphics yourselves?

Yes. Printing is done in-house.

What file format should my logo be?

A vector file such as AI, EPS or PDF is best, because it stays sharp at any size.

What is the difference between fabric and vinyl graphics?

Fabric stretches over a frame for a seamless, reusable look. Vinyl is applied directly to a surface and suits custom walls and shapes.

Will printed colours match my brand colours?

Printed colours can differ from screen colours. Share a reference for critical colours so we can check them.

Can you fit graphics to a stall someone else built?

Yes, if you share panel sizes and the fixing method. We will confirm what we can supply.
