What the basic package covers

A starting price is not a bare-bones part of the job. It is a total rate covering design, fabrication, graphics printing, installation, execution and dismantling, including power, furniture, LED screens and transport to other cities, all handled by our own teams, so you are not stitching together separate suppliers.

Why prices are starting points

Two stalls of the same size can differ widely in cost, because price follows decisions, not just area. These are the levers, roughly from biggest to smallest. Power, furniture, LED screens and transport to other cities are not extra line items: they are included.

  • Size and number of open sides: more floor area and more visible sides mean more build and more finish.
  • Structural complexity: curves, raised floors, hanging elements and second levels need more engineering and labour than flat walls.
  • Build method: shell, modular, custom and hybrid builds trade cost against freedom. See custom vs modular.
  • Graphics: lit and three-dimensional elements generally cost more than flat printed ones.
  • How elaborate the features are: a bespoke counter, feature wall or lit sign takes more design and labour than a standard one.

How to compare two stall quotes

  1. Check that both quote the same size and brief.
  2. Confirm what is included: design, fabrication, printing, installation, execution and dismantling.
  3. Ask whether power, furniture, LED screens and transport to other cities are included or priced on top. In ours they are included.
  4. Compare payment terms. Ours are 60% upfront and 40% on installation and complete setup.
  5. Ask who actually builds the stall. We do everything in-house.

The budget beyond the stall

The stall is one line in an exhibition budget. Also plan for the organiser's space fee, power and internet charges at the venue, staff travel and accommodation, printed material, giveaways and a contingency. Our planning guide shows where each fits.
