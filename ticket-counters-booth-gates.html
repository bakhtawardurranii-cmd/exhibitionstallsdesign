How do you size a counter or gate for your crowd?

Queues form when arrivals per minute exceed the counter's check-in speed, so we plan from the crowd, not from the space.

  1. Estimate arrivals at the busiest hour from your expected attendance and opening times.
  2. Time one check-in: ticket scan, badge print or name lookup. Slower checks need more counters.
  3. Count staff positions and leave room behind the counter for them to move.
  4. Reserve queue space in front, with a gate or barrier that guides people into a single line.
  5. Plan power and equipment for scanners, printers and screens.

What we need from you

  • Event name, city and venue, and the entrance layout
