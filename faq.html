Exhibition Stall Questions Answered, City by City

Real worries, short answers. From Karachi humidity to Lahore heat, Islamabad access and Multan dust, here is how we handle what exhibitors ask us most.

In short: These are the questions exhibitors in Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad actually worry about: climate, traffic, deadlines, cost surprises and show-day problems. Each answer comes from how we build, with starting prices in PKR, 60/40 payment and quotes within 24 hours.

Karachi

Will humidity ruin my exhibition stall in Karachi?

Not if it is built for it. Humid, salt-laden air can make unsealed boards swell, lift adhesives and corrode bare fixings, so we seal every panel face and edge, use corrosion-resistant fixings and laminate graphics against moisture. Read our Karachi stall designer page.

How do you handle Karachi traffic on build-up day?

We plan around it. Deliveries are timed outside peak traffic and loaded in build order, and a crew of 5 to 10 people typically installs a stall in about three days. See installation and dismantling.

Can I get a stall built in Karachi at short notice?

Often yes, but design sign-off must reach us at least one week before the event, and larger or custom stalls need much longer. Start with our planning guide.

Can I visit your Karachi workshop?

Our workshop is for fabrication, not public visits. We meet online or at a mutually agreed public place, and you can see work in progress by video. See contact.

How much do customized kiosk manufacturers charge in Karachi?

Kiosks start from PKR 15,000 as a total installed rate, covering design, fabrication, printing, installation, execution and dismantling. See our customized kiosk manufacturers page.

What should I ask a Karachi stall designer before I hire?

Ask how panels are sealed against humidity, who builds on site and how many people, how deliveries avoid traffic, what the price includes and who supports the stall once the show opens. Our answers are on the Karachi stall designer page.

Lahore

Does summer heat damage exhibition stalls in Lahore?

It can if the stall is not planned for it. Heat can loosen adhesives, expand acrylic and fabric and shorten the life of unventilated LED drivers. We leave expansion allowance, ventilate electronics and plan power load at design stage. See Lahore booth designer.

Will my logo be visible in a large hall?

It will if it is designed for distance. Most visitors first see your stall from the end of an aisle, so test the fascia at thumbnail size and favour high contrast over fine detail. Learn more in our stall design guide.

Do you have a workshop in Lahore, or do you ship from Karachi?

We have a local workshop and team in Lahore, so your stall is designed and built close to the show. See all locations.

Who fixes problems during a show in Lahore?

Our team offers show-day support, so a light, panel or graphic can be fixed while the exhibition runs. See installation.

Is a 10x20 stall enough for a Lahore show?

If you need a display, a counter and a small meeting zone, usually yes. A 10x20 starts from PKR 25,000 and a 20x20 from PKR 50,000. Compare them on our stall types page.

What does a Lahore booth designer actually plan?

Exhibition booth designing in Lahore plans for heat, large-hall sightlines and power load, then checks the design at three distances: aisle end, mid-aisle and arm's length. See the Lahore booth designer page.

Islamabad

Can you set up conferences and seminars in Islamabad?

Yes. We build branded backdrops, desks and display elements for conferences, seminars and hotel events through our local team. See event setup.

How do I make my branding look good on camera?

Use matte finishes, large simple logos and sensible lighting angles. Glossy panels throw glare into lenses and fine patterns can shimmer on video. See Islamabad booth designer.

What if the venue needs crew details or ID in advance?

Share the requirements as soon as you have them and we plan crews and timing around them. Early information avoids delays on build-up day. See our exhibition booth checklist.

Can you build a premium stall that is not flashy?

Yes. In formal settings restraint reads as authority, so we favour calm palettes, generous space and one clear message. See stall design.

Can you make ticket counters or registration desks in Islamabad?

Yes. We design, build and install branded counters, desks and gates. See ticket counters and booth gates.

How is exhibition booth designing different in Islamabad?

Formal audiences and constant cameras favour restraint: matte finishes, large simple logos and quiet lighting, with early planning for venue access. See the Islamabad booth designer page.

Multan

How do I get the most from a small budget in Multan?

Spend where eyes land: the fascia, the eye-level band of the back wall and the counter. A compact stall with three well-made zones beats a larger one spread thin. Basic 10x10 packages start from PKR 13,000. See stall costs.

Will dust and dry heat spoil a stall?

Dust shows quickly on dark matte finishes, so lighter or textured, washable surfaces forgive neglect. Cover displays between visits and give LED drivers airflow. See Multan stall designer.

Do you have a workshop in Multan?

We do not run a workshop in Multan. Our workshops are in Karachi, Islamabad and Lahore, and we design for and deliver to Multan exhibitions, with transport included in our starting prices.

Is a kiosk enough for a regional show?

Often yes. A kiosk concentrates your message and starts from PKR 15,000 as a total installed rate. See customized kiosks.

Can I get a reusable stall in Multan?

Yes. An Octanorm stall, which is modular and reconfigurable, starts from PKR 18,000. Read custom vs modular to choose.

What does a Multan stall designer do on a small budget?

Concentrates spend on the fascia, the eye-level wall and the counter, and specifies washable surfaces for dust. A basic 10x10 package starts from PKR 13,000. See the Multan stall designer page.

Anywhere in Pakistan

What hidden costs should I watch for in a stall quote?

Ask whether printing, installation, dismantling, power, furniture, LED screens and transport are included. In our starting prices they are, as total installed rates. See exhibition stall cost.

Is it safe to pay upfront?

Ask for the scope in writing first. Our terms are 60% upfront and 40% on installation and complete setup, and your written quote confirms exactly what is included. See our terms.

What if the design is not what I imagined?

You approve a 3D render before anything is built, and we do not cap revisions. We keep refining until you are satisfied, and most designs are finalised within a couple of revisions. See 3D booth rendering.

How late can I confirm my design?

At least one week before the event is our minimum. Larger stalls need far longer, so earlier is always safer.

What happens if something breaks during the show?

Our team offers show-day support, so problems can be fixed while the exhibition runs.

Who actually builds my stall?

Our own teams. Design, fabrication, graphics printing, installation and dismantling are all in-house, with local workshops in Karachi, Islamabad and Lahore. See in-house vs outsourced.

Can you build for a show outside your three workshop cities?

Share the venue and dates and we will confirm how we deliver and install. Our starting prices include transport to other cities. See get a quote.
