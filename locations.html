Where Do You Need an Exhibition Stall Designer?

Local workshops and on-ground teams in three cities, so design, fabrication and installation happen close to your event.

Multan exhibition stall designer

Which workshop will build your stall?

Match the workshop to your show. Exhibiting in Karachi, including inside Expo Centre Karachi? Our head workshop and a Karachi stall designer handle design to installation. For a Lahore show, a Lahore booth designer plans for summer heat and long hall sightlines, and fabrication happens in the basement warehouse of our Gulberg building. For formal conferences and institutional events, an Islamabad booth designer pairs booth design with event setup.

Exhibiting in Multan? We do not run a workshop there, but a Multan stall designer from our team designs for Multan shows and delivers the stall, with transport included in our starting prices.
