How Do You Book an Exhibition Stall in Pakistan, and What Will It Cost?

Two different bills sit behind every exhibition stall. One is the organiser's fee for the floor space. The other is the cost of designing and building what stands on it. Mixing them up is an easy way to misjudge a budget.

In short: To book an exhibition stall in Pakistan, you reserve floor space with the show's organiser and pay their space fee. You then pay a builder for the stall itself. We build stalls, we do not sell space, and our starting prices from PKR 13,000 cover design, fabrication, printing, installation and dismantling.

Two bills: the space and the stall

If you searched for "stall booking price" or "exhibition fees", this is the distinction that answers it. The organiser charges for floor space. A builder charges for the stall on top of it.

How to book an exhibition stall, step by step

  1. Find the show. Start with the organiser of the event you want. For Karachi, our Expo Centre Karachi schedule lists what is coming.
  2. Ask for the exhibitor manual and the space rates. The manual carries the rules; the rate card carries the price.
  3. Choose your position. Inline, corner, peninsula and island positions open different numbers of sides. Our guide to what an exhibition stall is explains each one.
  4. Confirm what the space includes. Bare space and shell scheme packages are very different starting points.
  5. Get the booking in writing. You want the stall number, size, open sides and build-up window on paper.
  6. Send us the manual. Design sign-off is needed at least one week before the event, and larger or custom stalls need much longer.

Questions to ask the organiser before you pay

  • Is this bare space or a shell scheme stall, and what is included?
  • What is the stall number, size and number of open sides?
  • What are the height and structure limits?
  • What power load is available, and what does the connection cost?
  • When are the build-up and exit windows?
  • Are there rules on fascia signs, hanging elements or second levels?
  • What are the payment and cancellation terms?

Bare space or shell scheme: what you still need to pay for

A shell scheme stall arrives with standard walls and a name fascia, so your spend is branding and extras. Bare space arrives empty, so you need a designed and built stall. Our stall types and starting prices page shows both routes. A shell stall starts at PKR 15,000 from us, an Octanorm stall at PKR 18,000 and a 10x10 basic package at PKR 13,000, all installed.

Exhibiting often? A reusable system can work out cheaper over several shows. See custom or modular exhibition booth.

Where to look for shows and organisers

Booking is done show by show, not city by city, so there is no single place for stall booking near me. Stall organisers and exhibition organisers are the same people: check the organiser named on the event page, and use the Trade Development Authority of Pakistan for its published exhibitions. Once you hold a space, our workshops in Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad can build for it, and we deliver to other cities including Multan.

Common questions

How much does it cost to book a stall at an exhibition in Pakistan?

The organiser sets the space fee for each show, venue and position, so it varies. We do not publish it because we do not set it. Ask the organiser for the rate card, then budget separately for the stall itself.

Is your starting price the stall booking price?

No. Our starting prices, from PKR 13,000, are for designing, building, installing and dismantling the stall. The booking price is the organiser's fee for the floor space.

Do you handle expo stall booking for me?

No. We are stall designers and fabricators, not organisers. You book space with the show's organiser, and we build the stall for it.

What is the difference between stall charges and exhibition fees?

Stall charges are what a builder charges for the stall. Exhibition fees are what the organiser charges for space and venue services. They are separate invoices.

Can I book a stall for one day?

That depends on the organiser, who decides the show days and space terms. Our prices are totals per event of up to three days, so a short show fits within them. For longer or more complex events, contact us for customized pricing.

Can you build a stall for space I have already booked?

Yes. Send your stall number, size and the exhibitor manual, and we design to the organiser's height, power and structure rules.

Can I rent a stall from you for the space I booked?

Yes. Once you hold the space, we can rent you a stall for the event or sell you one. See exhibition stall rental in Pakistan.
