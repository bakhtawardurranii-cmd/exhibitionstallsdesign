Customized Kiosk Manufacturers: Kiosks Built to Sell in a Small Space

Kiosks start small and must work hard. We design, manufacture, print and install them under one roof, so what is drawn is what stands in your mall, show or store.

In short: We are customized kiosk manufacturers in Pakistan. We design, build, print and install kiosks in-house from local teams in Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad, with prices from PKR 15,000 as a total installed rate.

What customized kiosk manufacturers actually do

A kiosk is a whole shop in a few square metres, so the work spans design, carpentry, electrics, graphics and installation. We do all of it in our own teams: design and 3D render, fabrication, graphics printing, test assembly in the workshop where the project calls for it, installation and dismantling. That means one contact, fewer hand-offs and quick changes.

Seven decisions that define a good kiosk

  1. Footprint and rules: the size limit and any restrictions on height, fixing and timing.
  2. Approach direction: which sides visitors will come from, and so which faces carry the brand.
  3. Power and lighting: how many points, and heat from lights and screens.
  4. Storage: stock, samples and staff belongings need somewhere hidden.
  5. Counter height and layout: comfortable for staff and customers.
  6. Mobility: permanent, or knock-down and reusable.
  7. Surface durability: what handling, cleaning and weather the finish must survive.
