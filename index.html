Which stall type is right for you?

A kiosk suits product sampling, retail and small activations where you need one compact, branded point. A shell scheme stall is a basic pre-built package with standard walls and a fascia, and your branding goes on top. An Octanorm stall uses a modular aluminium frame with panels you can reconfigure and reuse across shows. An island booth is open on all four sides, so it draws visitors from every aisle. A double-deck booth adds an upper level for meetings and hosting clients when you have the space and budget. Not sure which fits? Send us your show and budget and we will recommend one, or compare every stall type and size.

Who we build stalls for

We work with companies exhibiting at trade shows, expos and corporate events across Pakistan. That includes first-time exhibitors who need a simple branded stall and established brands that want a custom, island or double-deck booth. If you are unsure what you need, send your show details and stall size and we will tell you what works within your budget.

Questions people ask us

What is Exhibition Stalls Designs?

Exhibition Stalls Designs is a Pakistani exhibition stall and event setup company, operating since 2020. We design, 3D-render, fabricate, print, install and dismantle stalls with our own in-house teams.

Where do you work?

Our head workshop is in Karachi, with workshops and teams in Lahore and Islamabad. We build and install stalls for exhibitions across Pakistan, including Multan.

How much does an exhibition stall cost in Pakistan?

Starting prices run from PKR 13,000 for a basic 10x10 package to PKR 100,000 for a double-deck booth, installed. The price table above lists every stall type. Your final quote depends on stall size, design, materials, lighting and graphics, and our exhibition stall cost guide explains what moves the price up or down.

Do you do everything in-house?

Yes. Design, fabrication, graphics printing, installation and dismantling are all handled by our own teams, and, where the project calls for it, we test-assemble stalls in the workshop before they ship.

How fast do you quote, and how do payments work?

We send a tailored quote within 24 hours. Payment is 60% upfront and 40% once your stall is installed and set up.

What does an exhibition stall designer do?

An exhibition stall designer plans the layout, traffic flow, branding and structure of a stall, produces 3D renders for approval, and, in our case, builds and installs the stall. We design and build in-house, so what is drawn is what gets made.

Do you only build exhibition stalls?

No. We are also customized kiosk manufacturers across Pakistan, with workshops in Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad, and we build ticket counters, booth gates, mall standees and conference, seminar and outdoor event setups.

Can you build a stall that follows the organiser's rules?

Yes. We build shell scheme and Octanorm stalls as well as fully custom builds. Send us your exhibitor manual with your enquiry so we can design to your organiser's stall size, height and power rules.

How long does it take to build a stall?

Installation usually takes about three days with a crew of five to ten people. We need design sign-off at least one week before your event, so tell us your show date when you ask for a quote and we confirm the timeline in writing.

Do I get to see the stall before it is built?

Yes. We prepare a 3D render of your stall for your approval before fabrication starts, and revisions are not capped.
