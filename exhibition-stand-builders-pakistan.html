Exhibition Stand Builders in Pakistan: One In-House Team from Design to Dismantling

Looking for exhibition stand builders in Pakistan? Whether you call it a stand, a stall or a booth, we design it, build it in our own workshops and install it ourselves.

In short: We are exhibition stand builders in Pakistan, with workshops in Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad. One in-house team designs, fabricates, prints, installs and dismantles your stall. Starting prices run from PKR 13,000 for a 10x10 basic package to PKR 100,000 for a double-deck booth, installed.

Stand, stall or booth: what is the difference?

None, in practice. Exhibitors in Pakistan say stall, booth or stand for the same thing: the space and structure you use at a trade show.

That is why people search for exhibition stand builders, stall builders, stall makers, stall vendors and booth designers when they need the same job done. Our what is an exhibition stall guide explains the terms and the types.

What exhibition stand builders do

A stand builder plans, makes and installs the stall your brand shows in. With us that is one chain, handled by our own teams:

  • Design: layouts and 3D renders, covered in exhibition stall design and 3D booth rendering.
  • Fabrication: expo stall fabrication in our own CNC workshops.
  • Branding: graphics printed in-house and fitted to the stall.
  • Installation: our own crew builds on site and dismantles after the show.

How a stand project runs

  1. Enquiry: send your show, city and stall size.
  2. Quote: a tailored quote within 24 hours.
  3. Design: a 3D render for approval, with uncapped revisions. Sign-off is needed at least one week before the event.
  4. Build: in-house fabrication and printing, with test assembly where the project calls for it.
  5. Install: a crew of 5 to 10, typically about three days, then dismantling after the show.

Payment is 60% upfront and 40% once the stall is installed and set up.

Common questions

What does an exhibition stand builder do?

An exhibition stand builder designs the stand, fabricates it, prints and fits the graphics, installs it in the hall and dismantles it after the show. We do all of those steps ourselves.

Are stand builders, stall makers and booth designers the same thing?

In everyday use, yes. People use stand, stall and booth for the same space. We design, build and install, so we cover all of those searches.

How much do exhibition stand builders charge in Pakistan?

Our starting prices run from PKR 13,000 for a basic 10x10 package to PKR 100,000 for a double-deck booth, installed. Final quotes depend on size, design, materials and extras.

Which cities do you build in?

We have workshops and teams in Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad, and we deliver to exhibitions across Pakistan, including Multan.

Do you build custom and modular stands?

Yes. We build custom, modular and hybrid stalls, plus shell scheme and Octanorm stalls that follow organiser rules.

Do you sell exhibition space?

No. Organisers sell the floor space. We build the stand that stands on it.
