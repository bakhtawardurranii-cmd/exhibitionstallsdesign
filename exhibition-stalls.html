Common questions

What are the main types of exhibition stalls?

The main types are kiosks, basic 10x10 packages, larger sizes such as 10x20 and 20x20, shell scheme stalls, Octanorm stalls, island booths and double-deck booths.

How much does an exhibition stall cost in Pakistan?

Starting prices are PKR 13,000 for a basic 10x10 package, PKR 25,000 for a 10x20, PKR 50,000 for a 20x20, PKR 15,000 for a kiosk or shell stall, PKR 18,000 for an Octanorm stall, PKR 50,000 for an island and PKR 100,000 for a double-deck. These are total installed rates, and your quote depends on your requirements.

What is the difference between a shell scheme and an Octanorm stall?

A shell scheme is a basic, pre-built stall package that is quick and affordable. An Octanorm stall uses a modular aluminium system that can be reconfigured and reused, with more finish options.

What is an island booth?

An island booth is open on all four sides with aisles around it and no neighbouring stalls. It gives maximum visibility but needs careful design because every side must look like the front.

Do you build double-deck stalls?

Yes, we have built double-deck stalls. They need more planning time and early checks on venue rules for height, loading and safety.

Can I customise any of these stalls?

Yes. Every type is a starting point, and we adapt layout, graphics, lighting and features to your brand and space.
