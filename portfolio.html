How do you design an 18 × 6 m island stall around a laser and a crowd of 300?

A textile exhibitor needed an island for Textile Asia 2026 at Karachi Expo Centre, Hall 3: twelve 3 × 3 m stalls (108 m²), a laser and chiller drawing 17.7 kW at 400 V three-phase, a team of up to 40 and 300 or more visitors at peak.

  • Two closed walls (18 m north, 6 m west) carry the brand, and storage sits inside the hollow north wall instead of a separate store room.
  • Three zones, west to east: lounge and video wall, reception and meetings, then the laser running behind a 4.6 × 3.5 m glass rail under a logo frame at 4.57 m.
  • Power: about 26 A per phase, so the plan asks the organiser for at least 32 A three-phase.
  • Floor load: the laser is roughly 390 kg per m² and the chiller roughly 1,900 kg per m², so a load-spreading plate goes under the chiller.
  • Capacity: about 63 m² stays open, roughly 40 to 60 people at once, so timed demonstrations manage the crowd.

This is a concept for approval. A fabricator, an electrician and the organiser must check dimensions, loads, electrical work and fire rules before construction. For island options see island booth designs and prices, and for pricing see exhibition stall cost in Pakistan.

How do you design an instant-photo kiosk that fits in a car?

The Photo Totem is a portable kiosk design: a 1.6 m slim, arch-topped column with a 15.6 in portrait touch screen, HD camera with ring light, built-in printer, computer and battery. Guests tap, pose and take a finished 4x6 in print in about a minute, with no app or sign-up.

  • Footprint: a 500 × 400 mm base plate, about 28 kg in total.
  • Transport: three parts (base plate, lower and upper section) that pack into two flight cases.
  • Set-up: place the base, stand the lower section, lift the upper section onto the pegs, close two latches and switch on.
  • Branding: logo, event name, welcome message, colours, countdown and prints per session can be customised.

See the interactive 3D model of the Photo Totem, or read how we work as customized kiosk manufacturers.
