Terms and Conditions: Quotes, Payments and Design Approval

Last updated: October 2026. These terms apply to quotes and services from Exhibition Stalls Designs and to use of this website.

Quotes and pricing

Prices shown on this website are starting guides, not fixed offers. Each project is quoted individually after we discuss your requirements. We aim to send quotes within 24 hours. All prices are in Pakistani rupees (PKR) and are total rates for the installed stall unless the quote states otherwise.

What a quote can include

Our starting prices cover design, fabrication, graphics printing, installation, execution and dismantling, including power, furniture, LED screens and transport to other cities. Your written quote confirms the exact scope for your stall, and the final figure depends on the size, structure and finish you choose.

Payment

Unless agreed otherwise in writing, payment is 60% upfront to begin work and 40% on installation and complete setup.

Design approval and timelines

We prepare designs and 3D renders for your approval. We do not cap revisions: we keep refining until you are satisfied, and most designs are finalised within a couple of revisions. Final design approval must reach us at least one week before the event. Late approval or changes may affect delivery and cost.

Changes

Changes to scope after approval may change the price and timeline. We will confirm any change with you before proceeding.

Client responsibilities

You are responsible for giving accurate event details, brand files and content, and for sharing venue rules, access times and requirements that affect the stall.

Cancellation and refunds

Cancellation and refund terms are set out in your written quote or agreement. Work already done and materials already bought may not be refundable.

Intellectual property

Designs, renders and drawings we create remain our work until they are fully paid for. You may use content and brand material you supply, and you confirm you have the right to use it.

Liability

We take care in design, fabrication and installation. To the extent the law allows, our liability for any project is limited to the amount you paid for it, and we are not liable for indirect or consequential losses, or for delays caused by events outside our control, including venue or organiser decisions.
