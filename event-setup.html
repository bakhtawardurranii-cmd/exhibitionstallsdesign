Beyond exhibitions

Event Setup for Conferences, Seminars and Outdoor Events

We design, build and set up branded event spaces in hotels, venues and open-air locations, using the same in-house teams that build our exhibition stalls. Event setup on the go.

What we set up

Conferences and seminars

Branded setups for hotel and venue events, planned around your room and schedule.

Hotel events

On-site build and branding that fits hotel access and timing rules.

Outdoor events

Structures and branded elements built for open-air use.

Branded elements

Backdrops, welcome signage, counters and display units, designed and printed in-house.

Local teams

Workshops and crews in Karachi, Islamabad and Lahore.

Setup and dismantling

We install before the event and clear up afterwards.

Activation idea

For weddings, launches and corporate events, a portable instant-photo kiosk gives guests a branded print to take home. See our Photo Totem design.

How it works

  1. Enquiry

    Tell us the event, city and what you need.

  2. Quote

    A tailored quote within 24 hours.

  3. Design

    We design it and show a 3D render for approval.

  4. Build

    Fabrication and printing in our own workshops.

  5. Install

    Our crew sets up on site and dismantles after.

Design sign-off is needed at least one week before the event, and earlier is better. Payment is 60% upfront and 40% on installation and complete setup. See our terms.

What we need from you

  • Event name, date, city and venue
  • Approximate floor area and audience size
  • Your logo, brand colours and any text for signage
  • Venue access times and any rules we should know
  • What you want built, or just the goal and we will suggest ideas

Common questions

What kinds of events do you set up?

We set up conferences, seminars, hotel events and outdoor events, as well as exhibitions.

Which cities do you cover?

We have local workshops and teams in Karachi, Islamabad and Lahore.

Do you design and print the branding?

Yes. Design, 3D rendering, fabrication, printing and installation are all done in-house.

We send a tailored quote within 24 hours of your enquiry.
