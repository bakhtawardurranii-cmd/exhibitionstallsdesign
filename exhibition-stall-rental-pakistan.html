What a stall rental price covers

Each price is a total for one event of up to three days. It covers:

  • Stall design and a 3D render for your approval
  • Fabrication and graphics printing
  • Installation, execution and dismantling
  • Power, furniture, LED screens and transport to other cities

Payment is 60% upfront and 40% once the stall is installed and set up.

Customized pricing for your event

A starting price is a starting point. The final figure moves with the event itself:

  • How many days the event runs, if more than three
  • Venue and city, and the organiser's build-up rules
  • Stall size and open sides
  • Design complexity, lighting and screens
  • How tight the timeline is

How to rent a stall, step by step

  1. Send your event, city and stall size. Add the organiser's exhibitor manual if you have it.
  2. Receive a tailored quote within 24 hours.
  3. Approve the 3D render. Design sign-off is needed at least one week before the event, and earlier for custom stalls.
  4. Pay 60% upfront to start fabrication.
  5. Our crew installs the stall, typically in about three days, and supports you on show days.
  6. Pay the remaining 40% on installation, then we dismantle after the event.

Renting a stall is separate from booking floor space. For that, read exhibition stall booking and fees.

Common questions

Do you rent exhibition stalls in Pakistan?

Yes. We rent and sell stalls. Rented stalls are mostly built on site inside the expo centre of each city, and we dismantle them after the event.

How much does it cost to rent a stall?

Starting prices run from PKR 13,000 for a 10x10 ft basic package to PKR 100,000 for a double-deck booth. Each is a total per event of up to three days, and your final price depends on the event.

How many days does the rental price cover?

Up to three days per event. If your event runs longer or has special requirements, contact us for customized pricing.

Is stall rental the same as stall booking?

No. Booking is reserving floor space with the show's organiser. Rental is the stall itself, designed, built and installed by us on that space.

Can you deliver a rented stall outside Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad?

Yes. Transport to other cities, including Multan, is included in our starting prices.

Can I rent a stall for a conference or promotion instead of an exhibition?

Yes. We build promotional, brand and conference stalls for events. See our event setup page.
