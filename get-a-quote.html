Common questions

What does a stall cost?

Starting prices: kiosk from PKR 15,000, 10x10 basic package from PKR 13,000, 10x20 from PKR 25,000, 20x20 from PKR 50,000, shell stall from PKR 15,000, Octanorm stall from PKR 18,000, island from PKR 50,000 and double-deck from PKR 100,000. Each figure is a total installed rate, and the final price depends on your requirements.

How do payments work?

60% upfront and 40% on installation and complete setup.

How long before the event should I confirm the design?

Design sign-off is needed at least one week before the event. Larger or custom stalls benefit from much more time.

What should you send for the most accurate quote?

  • Event name, city, venue and dates
  • Stall size and how many sides are open
  • Your logo files and brand colours
  • What visitors should do at the stall: sample, demo, sign up or meet
  • Any machines, screens or heavy items that need space or power
