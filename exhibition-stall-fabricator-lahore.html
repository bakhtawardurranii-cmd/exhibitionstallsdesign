Fabricating for Lahore heat

A stall in a hot hall is part of a thermal system. When we fabricate in Lahore we plan for it:

  • Adhesives and vinyl: heat can make adhesives creep and vinyl edges lift, so we choose heat-aware adhesives.
  • Acrylic and panels: acrylic expands and needs room to move, so we leave expansion gaps.
  • Fabric: tension shifts with temperature, so it is specified and fitted with that in mind.
  • LED power supplies: they need ventilation or they age early, so drivers are placed where air can reach them.
  • Dark finishes and screens: both add heat, so power and finishes are budgeted before the show, not during it.

Built to read in a big hall

Most visitors first meet your stall from the end of an aisle. We judge a design from three places before it goes to the workshop: from the aisle end (can the name and one message be read?), mid-aisle (does the counter or screen explain what you do?) and at arm's length (are the finishes worth touching?).

Fabrication then protects what the design intends: fascia height, contrast and clean joins. See our Lahore stall designers page for the design side.

How a Lahore fabrication job runs

  1. Enquiry: send your show, stall size and the organiser's exhibitor manual.
  2. Quote: a tailored quote within 24 hours.
  3. Design: a 3D render for your approval, with uncapped revisions. Sign-off is needed at least one week before the event.
  4. Build: fabrication and printing in our Gulberg warehouse, with test assembly where the project calls for it.
  5. Install: our crew of 5 to 10 builds on site, typically in about three days, then dismantles after the show.

How does Lahore heat affect stall fabrication?

Heat can loosen adhesives, expand panels and acrylic, change fabric tension and shorten the life of poorly ventilated LED drivers. We plan expansion gaps, ventilation and power load at design stage.

How much does a stall cost in Lahore?

Starting prices are PKR 13,000 for a basic 10x10 package and PKR 25,000 for a 10x20 booth, installed. Custom builds are quoted to the design.
