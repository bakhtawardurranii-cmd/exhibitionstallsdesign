Common questions

What services do you offer?

We offer exhibition stall design, fabrication, 3D rendering, installation and dismantling, branding and printing, plus event setup, mall kiosks and standees, and ticket counters and booth gates.

Can I book just one service?

Yes. You can book a single service or let us handle the whole project, all done in-house.

Where do you work?

We have local workshops and teams in Karachi, Islamabad and Lahore.

How do the services fit together?

Most exhibitors start with exhibition booth designing, approve the stall in 3D booth rendering, then move to exhibition stall fabrication and branding and printing, and finish with booth installation and dismantling. Because one in-house team does every step, the stall you approve is the stall that gets built.

Planning a kiosk, a conference or an event entrance instead? See customized kiosk manufacturers, event setup and ticket counters and booth gates. For budgets, read exhibition stall cost in Pakistan.
