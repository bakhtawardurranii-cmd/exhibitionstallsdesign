Exhibition Stand Builders in Karachi: Stands Built for Expo Centre Karachi Shows

Exhibiting in Karachi means booking space with the organiser and then finding a stand builder you can trust. This page explains how the two fit together and what a Karachi stand builder should do for you.

In short: We are exhibition stand builders in Karachi. One in-house team designs, builds, prints, installs and dismantles your stand, including inside Expo Centre Karachi. Starting prices run from PKR 13,000 for a 10x10 basic package, installed, and we reply with a tailored quote within 24 hours.

Two bills: the space and the stand

When you exhibit at Expo Centre Karachi, you pay the organiser for floor space and then pay a stand builder for what stands on it. They are separate. We build the stand and do not sell space. Read how stall booking and fees work before you compare budgets.

Shell scheme, bare space or a custom stand?

Shows usually offer either a shell scheme (standard walls and a fascia) or bare space where you bring your own stand. The choice changes what you need from a builder:

  • Shell scheme: the lowest cost and effort. Your branding goes on top, and we can upgrade the look with graphics, lighting and furniture.
  • Octanorm: a modular aluminium frame with panels you can reuse and reconfigure, good if you exhibit often. See modular exhibition booths.
  • Custom stand: built once to your design, for flagship shows, islands and double-deck stands.

We build all three and follow your organiser's rules. Send the exhibitor manual with your enquiry so we design to the stand size, height and power limits.

Stand design that works in a big Karachi hall

In a large hall, visitors meet your stand from a distance. The stands that work in Karachi tend to share the same habits:

  • One hero message, readable from the aisle, instead of many competing products.
  • A counter placed where visitors naturally slow down.
  • Matt or controlled surfaces, because glossy finishes glare under hall lights.
  • Sealed finishes that cope with Karachi's damp, salt-laden air.
  • Design approved early. We need sign-off at least one week before the event.

Our Expo Centre Karachi exhibitor guide covers the venue and the exhibitor manual in more detail, and Karachi stall designer explains how humidity and traffic shape a design.

A worked example: an island for Textile Asia 2026, Hall 3

A textile exhibitor needed an island of twelve 3 x 3 m stalls (108 m2) at Karachi Expo Centre, with a laser and chiller drawing 17.7 kW at 400 V three-phase, a team of up to 40 and 300 or more visitors at peak.

The concept uses two closed walls to carry the brand, hides storage inside the hollow north wall, splits the space into three zones and asks the organiser for at least 32 A three-phase power. It is a concept for approval: a fabricator, an electrician and the organiser must check dimensions, loads, electrical work and fire rules before construction. Common questions

Are you exhibition stand builders in Karachi?

What is the difference between space booking and stand building?

The organiser sells floor space and sets its own fee. A stand builder designs and builds the stand on that space. We build stands and do not sell space.

Can you build to the organiser's rules?

Yes. Send the exhibitor manual with your enquiry so we design to the stand size, height and power rules. We build shell scheme and Octanorm stands as well as custom builds.
