Our story

Who Designs, Builds and Installs Your Stall? An In-House Team Since 2020

We have designed, built and installed exhibition stalls since 2020. One in-house team handles your booth from first sketch to final teardown.

Who we are

Exhibition Stalls Designs offers booth and stall setup, including design and fabrication, for exhibitions, conferences and seminars. Our designs are built to be carpenter-friendly, so what is drawn can be made well and made on time, and our teams handle the move from workshop to venue.

We also build kiosks, ticket counters, booth gates, mall standees and hotel or outdoor event setups. Our line is simple: event setup on the go.

Everything in-house

Design, 3D rendering, fabrication, graphics printing, installation and dismantling are all done by our own teams. That means one point of contact, fewer hand-offs and faster changes. Where a project calls for it, we test-assemble stalls in the workshop before they ship. Our designs are created by certified 3D artists who follow structural engineering best practice, so stalls are strong as well as good-looking.

Local workshops in three cities

We run local workshops and on-ground teams in Karachi, Islamabad and Lahore. We also serve Multan exhibitions from these workshops. Our head workshop is in Karachi. In Islamabad and Lahore, fabrication takes place in the warehouse in the basement of our office buildings.
