Modular Exhibition Booths in Pakistan: Reusable Stalls You Can Rent or Buy

If you exhibit more than once a year, a modular booth can save money and build time. This page explains how modular stalls work, what they cost and when custom is the better choice.

In short: Modular exhibition booths are reusable frame-and-panel stalls that you can reconfigure for different sizes and shows. Our Octanorm modular stalls start from PKR 18,000, installed, and we both rent and sell them. Choose modular if you exhibit often, and custom for a one-off flagship stall.

What is a modular exhibition booth?

A modular booth is built from a reusable system of frames and panels that can be reconfigured, rather than from parts cut once for one design. In Pakistan the most common version is the Octanorm system: white panels in an aluminium frame, a fascia with spotlights, and printed graphics fitted straight onto the panels. The same parts can be rebuilt in a different size or layout at your next show.

Modular booth design: what you can change

  • Graphics: printed panels fit straight onto the system, so a new show can mean new artwork, not a new stall.
  • Size and layout: panels and frames can be rebuilt for a different stall footprint.
  • Counters, lighting and carpet: added per show, with a lit fascia and spotlights as typical.
  • Custom elements: a feature wall or counter can be added to make a hybrid.

Rent or buy a modular booth

We both rent and sell stalls. Rental prices are totals per event of up to three days, so a rented stall suits a single show or a trial. Buying makes sense if you exhibit several times a year and want to reuse the system. Rented stalls are mostly built on site inside the expo centre, while stalls you buy outright are built in our workshops and, where the project calls for it, test-assembled once before shipping. Details are on exhibition stall rental in Pakistan.

How to compare modular and custom costs properly

Do not compare only the first invoice. Ask any builder to quote the same stall both ways, then add up the shows you expect over the next two or three years:

  1. The cost of the first show.
  2. The cost of new graphics, transport and any storage for each later show.
  3. The cost of rebuilding a custom stall that cannot be reused.

If you exhibit often, the modular total is usually lower. For a single flagship show, a custom build can cost a similar amount, and islands and double-deck stalls usually lean custom because of their size and structure.

Common questions

What is a modular exhibition stall?

A modular stall is built from reusable frames and panels, usually an aluminium-frame Octanorm system, that can be reconfigured for different stall sizes and layouts across shows.

Is a modular booth cheaper than a custom booth?

Often over several shows, because you reuse it. For a single show a simple custom build can cost a similar amount.

Can a modular booth look premium?

Yes. Good graphics, lighting and a few custom elements make a modular stall look polished.

Can I mix modular and custom?
