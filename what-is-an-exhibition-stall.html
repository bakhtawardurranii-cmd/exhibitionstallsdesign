What Is an Exhibition Stall, and Which Type Do You Need?

The vocabulary of exhibitions hides a simple idea. This guide decodes the terms, shows what a stall is made of, and explains how position and build method change what you get.

In short: An exhibition stall is the space, and the structure built in it, that a business uses at a trade show or exhibition to show products, meet visitors and gather leads. It is also called a booth or a stand.

Stall, booth or stand?

All three words mean the same thing, and exhibitor manuals use them interchangeably. Only habit differs: stall in South Asia, booth in North America, stand in the UK and Europe.

What a stall is made of

  • Floor space: the area you booked.
  • Walls or backdrop: carry your message and graphics.
  • Fascia: the name sign above the stall.
  • Counter or desk: where staff greet visitors and capture leads.
  • Displays: shelves, plinths or cabinets.
  • Lighting and power: spotlights, screens and sockets.

Types by position: how many sides are open?

TypeOpen sidesWhat it means
Inline1In a row between neighbours. Common and usually the most affordable.
Corner2At a row end. More visibility than inline.
Peninsula3Only the back is against another stall.
Island4Aisles all around. Maximum visibility and design effort.

Types by how they are built

  • Shell scheme: a basic pre-built package with standard walls and a name fascia. Quick and economical.
  • Octanorm modular: an aluminium-frame system that can be reconfigured and reused.
  • Custom: designed and built from scratch for one brand.

Glossary

Stall, booth, stand
Three names for the same thing: the space and structure an exhibitor uses at a show. 'Stall' is common in South Asia, 'booth' in North America, 'stand' in the UK and Europe.
Fascia
The header sign above a stall showing the exhibitor's name.
Build-up
The period when exhibitors install their stalls before a show opens.
Dismantling
Taking the stall down and clearing the space after the show.
Footprint
The floor area a stall occupies.
Turnkey
One supplier handling design, build and installation together.

Common questions

What is an exhibition stall?

An exhibition stall is the space and structure a business uses at a trade show to display products, meet visitors and generate leads.

Is a stall the same as a booth?

Yes. Stall, booth and stand all mean the same thing.

What is a shell scheme stall?

A basic pre-built stall package with standard walls and a name fascia. What is included varies by show.

What is an island stall?

A stall open on all four sides with aisles around it and no neighbours attached.
