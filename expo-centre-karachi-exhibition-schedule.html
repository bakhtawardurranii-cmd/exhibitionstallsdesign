Work backwards from the show date

  1. Get the exhibitor manual from the organiser as soon as you book. See how to book a stall and what it costs.
  2. Send us your brief and stall size, and approve the 3D render. Our ten-step planning guide shows the order.
  3. Finish design sign-off at least one week before the show, earlier for custom builds.
  4. Allow for installation, which typically takes about three days with a crew of 5 to 10, subject to the build-up window the organiser gives you.
  5. Use our exhibition booth checklist for build-up and show days.

What the show type means for your stall

  • Machinery and industrial shows (the May 2027 cluster, Textile Asia): ask about floor load and power before you design. Our Textile Asia 2026 island stall concept walks through those questions.
  • Consumer and gift shows: counters, product display and quick sampling matter most. A kiosk booth or compact 10x10 package often does the job.
  • Conference-style events: think meeting space and signage. A larger or double-deck booth can host buyers.

Venue facts, hall layout and Karachi logistics are in our Expo Centre Karachi exhibitor guide, and our Karachi stall designer and fabricator page explains how our head workshop plans for the city.

Where to confirm dates

The organiser of each show has the final word. For a government view of venue events, see the Trade Development Authority of Pakistan, which describes the venue in its Karachi Expo Centre profile. We build stalls, and we do not organise exhibitions or set show dates.

Common questions

What exhibitions are at Expo Centre Karachi in December 2026?

The Textile Asia group of shows is listed for 5 to 7 December 2026, and a cluster of consumer-goods shows including Gift Asia is listed for 24 to 26 December 2026. Confirm with each organiser, because dates can move.

When is My Karachi Expo 2027?

We could not find 2027 dates published. The KCCI MyKarachi exhibition last ran from 6 to 8 February 2026 at Karachi Expo Centre. Check the KCCI page for the next announcement.

Where can I find the TDAP exhibition calendar?

The Trade Development Authority of Pakistan publishes exhibition information on its website. Organisers remain the best source for exact dates, venues and space rates.

How early should I approve my stall design for a Karachi show?

We need design sign-off at least one week before the event. Larger or custom stalls need much more time, so start as soon as you hold your space.

Can you build a stall for a show next week?

Sometimes, for a basic stall. Send your show, stall number and size, and we tell you straight whether the timeline is realistic.

Do you organise exhibitions or sell space?

No. We design, fabricate, install and dismantle stalls. Organisers sell the space.
